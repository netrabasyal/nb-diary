# Privacy

## What NB Diary collects

| Data | Where it lives | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email address | Entra External ID only, not our database | Sign-in |
| Display name (optional), timezone | Our database | Personalisation, correct week boundaries |
| Workouts, sets, notes, measurements, preferences | Your device and our database | The app's purpose |
| Error reports | Application Insights, without personal data | Fixing bugs |

Not collected: date of birth, gender, location, contacts, advertising identifiers.

## Your controls (Phase 4 onwards)

- Export all your data as JSON and CSV.
- Delete your account: access ends at once, data is erased within 30 days.

## On this device

Workout data is stored in the browser's storage for NB Diary. Install the app to your home screen
so Safari doesn't clear it after 7 days without a visit.
